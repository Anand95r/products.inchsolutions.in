Privacy Policy

App: Inch Wealth · Developer: Inch Solutions, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India · Effective date: 14 November 2026

In short: Inch Wealth stores your financial data only on your device. Inch Solutions does not collect, receive, store or share your personal or financial data.

1. Who we are

Inch Wealth is a personal finance app developed by Inch Solutions, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (GSTIN 33CFRPR3498R1Z5). In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to Inch Solutions.

2. Information you enter in the app

To use Inch Wealth, you may enter information such as:

  • Investments and holdings (for example stocks, mutual funds, PPF and NPS)
  • Income, expenses, categories and budgets
  • Assets, liabilities and notes you add

This information is stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to any server operated by us.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or financial information from the app. Inch Wealth does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your bank, broker or investment account login details.

4. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect your data, we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

5. Device backups

Your phone's operating system may include app data in your device's own backup (for example, Android backup to your Google account), depending on your phone settings. Such backups are managed by Google under your account and are not accessible to us.

6. Future backup feature

We plan to offer an optional backup to your own Google account. If this feature is added, it will be optional, the backup will be stored in your Google account, and this policy will be updated before the feature is released to explain exactly what data is stored and how.

7. Data security

Your data is protected by your device's own security. We recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time by deleting entries in the app, clearing the app's data in your phone settings, or uninstalling the app. Since we do not hold a copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

Inch Wealth is intended for adults managing personal and family finances and is not directed at children.

10. Not investment advice

Inch Wealth is a tracking tool. It does not provide investment, tax or financial advice.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

12. Contact us

For any questions about this policy or the app, write to info@inchsolutions.in.
